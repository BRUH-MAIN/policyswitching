## Presentation: three switching controllers vs a matched generalist (Addendum 8)

Rohan stopped the project at simulation and asked for a presentation comparing the switching
controllers with a generalist. Built and ran, all pre-registered first (Addendum 8):

- GeneralistV3 (`SPEC=GeneralistV3`): v8b's config on flat + rough + stairs, from v8b model_1999,
  4,000 iterations, job 12816. Per-class row means logged.
- Classifier for the onboard switch retrained on real-height scans (job 12818, layouts 2000-2011).
- Evaluation, 24 layouts x 6 arms x 3 conditions x 256 trials (jobs 12838, 12850), with
  `a100/switch_follow_present_slurm.sh`; videos with `a100/present_videos_slurm.sh` (12852).

Result: tipping-only, everything with the v8b recipe is at ~99% (oracle switch minus generalist
+0.3, CI -0.1..+0.6). With any shin or body contact counted, oracle switching beats the generalist
by +30.3 in all 24 layouts. The onboard classifier deadlocks at 17 cm (findings #37), and the
switching arms lose ~6 points under 20 ms delay (old flat/rough specialists). Write-up:
report_content/presentation_analysis.md. Figures: report_content/figures/presentation/. Deck:
claude.ai artifact (private), link given to Rohan.
